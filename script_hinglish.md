## BEAT 1 — THE NUMBERS (0:45–2:00) 🔥 retention beat: the flip
2022 se pehle Russia Bharat ko lagbhag tel bechta hi nahi tha. Bharat ke total import ka sirf do percent. Phir West ne Moscow par sanctions lagaye, European buyers hat gaye, aur Russian oil ko turant ek naya ghar chahiye tha. Bharat ne hisaab lagaya. Financial year 2026 tak Bharat Russia se 40.8 billion dollar ka crude khareed raha tha. Total import ka 30.3 percent. Aur is July mein toh hadh hi ho gayi. 51.1 percent. Bharat ne jitna tel khareeda, uska aadhe se zyada Russia se aaya. Ye agle chhah suppliers ko milakar bhi zyada hai. UAE, Saudi Arabia, Venezuela, Brazil, Oman aur America, sabko jod lo, phir bhi Russia se kam.

[ON SCREEN: animated bar chart — 2% (2021) → 30.3% (FY2026) → 51.1% (July 2026); July 2026 pie chart]

## BEAT 2 — THE DISCOUNT (2:00–3:15) 🔥 retention beat: why so cheap
Toh wajah kya hai? Ek shabd: discount. Jab sanctions lage, Russian oil Europe nahi ja saka, toh Moscow ne use saste mein bechna shuru kar diya, jo bhi khareede. Ek waqt toh ye 35 dollar per barrel tak bik raha tha. Jis desh ko apni zaroorat ka 88 percent tel import karna padta hai, uske liye ye koi bargain nahi hai. Ye survival hai. Har sasta barrel Bharat ka import bill ghatata hai, inflation kabu mein rakhta hai, aur 1.4 billion logon ke liye fuel sasta rakhta hai.

[ON SCREEN: Brent vs discounted Russian crude spread chart; "$35" callout]

## BEAT 3 — THE LOOPHOLE (3:15–4:30) 🔥 retention beat: the reveal
Ab woh baat jo zyadatar log galat samajhte hain. Bharat sanctions nahi tod raha hai. Woh us darwaze se andar ja raha hai jo West ne khud khula chhoda tha. December 2022 mein Group of Seven ne Russian oil par price cap lagaya: 60 dollar per barrel. Idea clever tha. Tel behta rahe taaki global prices na faten, lekin Russia ki kamai cap ho jaye. Toh jab tak Bharat cap ke andar, non-Western shipping aur insurance ke saath khareedta hai, woh likhe hue rules ke hisaab se khel raha hai. Sanctions Moscow ki revenue nichodne ke liye the, tel rokne ke liye nahi. Bharat ne bas fine print padh liya.

[ON SCREEN: "$60 PRICE CAP" graphic; flow diagram Russia → tanker → Indian refinery, "cap ke andar = rules ke andar"]

## BEAT 4 — THE PRESSURE (4:30–5:45) 🔥 retention beat: escalation
Washington ne notice kiya. July 2025 mein America ne Indian goods par extra 25 percent tariff thok diya, saaf taur par Russian oil ki wajah se, jabki China, jo aur bhi zyada khareedta hai, bakhsh diya gaya. Woh tariff February 2026 mein wapas le liya gaya. Lekin phir bada kadam aaya. Is September mein President Trump ne Lindsey Graham sanctions act par sign kar diya. Ye unhe taakat deta hai Russian energy ke bade buyers par 100 percent tak tariff lagane ki. House ne ise 262 ke mukable 159 voton se pass kiya. Bharat duniya mein Russian crude ka dusra sabse bada buyer hai. Modi aur Trump ki phone par baat bhi hui, kuch din pehle. Delhi ka message nahi badla: hum nazar rakhe hue hain, aur 1.4 billion logon ki energy security ki raksha karenge.

[ON SCREEN: timeline — July 2025 "25% tariff" → Feb 2026 "wapas" → Sept 2026 "Graham Act signed"; red stamp "100% TAK"]

## BEAT 5 — WHY INDIA WON'T STOP (5:45–6:45) 🔥 retention beat: the core answer
Toh phir ruk kyun nahi jaate? Kyunki dusra raasta aur bura hai. Kal ko Russian oil band kar do, toh Bharat ko turant 20 lakh barrel roz ka intezaam karna hoga, aise bazaar mein jahan Brent 100 dollar ke upar hai aur Gulf mein apni dikkatin chal rahi hain. Import bill fat jayega. Inflation peeche aayegi. Aur ye woh twist hai jo Washington mein koi zor se nahi kehta: agar Bharat ne khareedna band kar diya, toh woh 20 lakh barrel gayab nahi honge. Global supply tight hogi, aur daam sabke liye badhenge, America samet. Duniya ko chupchaap zaroorat hai ki Bharat discounted Russian oil khareedta rahe. Yahi woh sachchai hai jo is poore khel ko thaame hue hai.

[ON SCREEN: "20 LAKH BARRELS/DAY" — supply shock ripple graphic]

## PAYOFF (6:45–7:10)
Toh wapas usi sawal par jisse shuru kiya tha. America Bharat ko saza kyun deta hai aur China ko kyun bakhshta hai? Kyunki Delhi par dabav dalna uske bas mein hai, aur Beijing se ladna uske bas mein nahi. Aur Bharat rukega kyun nahi? Kyunki 1.4 billion logon ke liye sasti energy koi policy choice nahi hai. Ye survival hai. Yahi poori kahani hai.
